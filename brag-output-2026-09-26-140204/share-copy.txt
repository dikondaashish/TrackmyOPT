TITLE
TrackMyOPT Chrome Extension — OPT Clocks, Filing Dates & Job Application Demo

DESCRIPTION
See where to find the TrackMyOPT extension tools and how to use them:
• OPT Clock and STEM Clock: enter an EAD start date and view the tracker.
• OPT and STEM filing dates: calculate a filing window and review the countdown.
• Daily reminders and case status in the extension popup.
• Prefill supported application fields, then review the result.
• Save jobs to Wishlist or mark them Applied.
• Review résumé keyword gaps, create a tailored résumé, and edit a cover letter.
• Manage saved answers, prefill modes, Guided Autopilot, and sidebar preferences.

Learn more: https://www.trackmyopt.com

Demonstration uses fictional data and illustrative service responses. Service waits are shortened. AI and reminder features may depend on your plan. Review all application content before submitting. Confirm official immigration guidance with your DSO. TrackMyOPT does not guarantee immigration or hiring outcomes.
