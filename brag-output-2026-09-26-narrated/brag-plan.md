# TrackMyOPT Chrome Web Store walkthrough

A 90-second, 1920 × 1080 product demo. This is the 90-second revision of the approved 162-second landscape walkthrough. All 42 original scenes, features, layouts, crops, and click/result sequences are retained. Holds and explanatory captions are tightened; the music stays at its normal speed.

## Product and creative decisions

1. Product: TrackMyOPT Chrome extension, with OPT/STEM tools and job-application assistance.
2. Audience: prospective extension users who need to see where controls are and what they do.
3. Strongest benefit: use saved profile data on an application while keeping review and submission in the user's hands.
4. Hook: real Prefill click and filled form within 1.4 seconds.
5. Proof: source-rendered popup, clocks, calculators, sidebar, dialogs, and Chrome side panel.
6. Features: four OPT tools, reminders, case status, prefill, saved answers, job saving, AI keyword analysis, résumé workflow, cover-letter editing, modes and sidebar settings.
7. Branding: repository logo, blue #2563eb, navy #0f172a, background #f6f8fb, native system typography. Source screen styling is retained.
8. Tone: polished, practical product walkthrough; explicit action/result captions.
9. Destination: YouTube video URL in the Chrome Web Store listing; no store submission in this task.

## Music cue guidance

Bundled Happy Beats Business Moves vol. 1 by ende.app; 163.96 seconds, 120.19 BPM. Cue preset shipped with composition. Interaction/readability determines edits; Gemini Charon narration and bundled music. Soft fade-in/out, understated click cues. No rendered speed or performance claims: service waits are explicitly shortened.

## Fidelity and boundaries

Capture scripts bundle the actual repository extension components. A local harness supplies fictional profile, account, calculator, tracker and AI-service responses; no account, applicant data, or production endpoint is accessed. Prefill is executed by the actual runPrefill engine. Calendar and date calculations run in the product code. UI state captures are held and edited for readability, not a continuous screen recording. AI numeric score is cropped out; no invented performance metric appears. Source generation wait times are shortened and labeled. Résumé side panel is the current Chrome route; cover-letter controls demonstrate the separate in-page résumé workflow. No app source changes.
