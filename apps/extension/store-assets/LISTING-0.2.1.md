## Detailed description

> Bring your job applications and OPT timeline into one browser workflow.
>
> TrackMyOPT helps F-1 students prepare applications, keep track of opportunities, and use OPT and STEM OPT tools alongside their TrackMyOPT account.
>
> PREPARE, REVIEW, AND TRACK
>
> • Prefill supported empty application fields using your saved job-portal profile and the resume prepared for the current job.
> • Prepare a job-specific resume, draft screening answers, and create a cover letter from your experience and the job description. AI output needs your review.
> • Save the current job with its company, role, link, and available posting details. The assistant checks whether a job is already in your tracker and can record a recognized application-success page.
> • View OPT/STEM filing windows and unemployment-day tools using the dates and employment information you save.
> • Choose Step-by-step filling, or use plan-supported Continuous filling and opt-in Guided Autopilot. Guided Autopilot may advance supported non-final steps and pauses for review; you submit the application yourself.
>
> HOW TO GET STARTED
>
> 1. Sign in to TrackMyOPT and add your job-portal prefill information.
> 2. Open a supported job posting or application. Use the page assistant or extension popup to prepare your resume, save the job, or start Prefill.
> 3. Review and edit every filled answer and attachment before continuing or submitting.
>
> CONTROL OVER YOUR INFORMATION
>
> Existing filled fields and uploaded files stay in place. Unsupported controls may remain blank. Optional saved private answers—including work authorization, compensation, date of birth, and EEO answers—can fill matching empty fields when you click Prefill, without another approval step.
>
> If you choose to save a default job-portal login, that same Prefill click can fill its email and password on a supported HTTPS top-level login or create-account form. Credential filling never clicks Login or Create Account. The employer or application site can read filled values before you submit.
>
> AI screening drafts use the current job description, screening question, and active resume. Saved private answers and portal credentials are not used to prompt AI. Review AI drafts for accuracy. Passwords, tokens, resumes, and answers are not stored in Chrome sync.
>
> WHY CHROME ACCESS IS NEEDED
>
> Storage keeps sign-in state, preferences, and working data. Tab and page access identify the current job and support requested prefill. Packaged scripts run the assistant on supported job and career pages, including supported application frames. The side panel keeps preparation and review tools beside the application. Notifications report relevant extension action results. TrackMyOPT website access supports sign-in and account APIs.
>
> A TrackMyOPT account is required. Features and usage limits depend on your plan; supported sites and form behavior can vary. Screenshots show the product tour with fictional sample data.
>
> TrackMyOPT never submits job applications. It is independent software, not a government service or legal adviser, and is not affiliated with USCIS, DHS, SEVP, ICE, employers, or applicant-tracking systems. Confirm immigration requirements with your DSO or qualified adviser.
>

## Privacy and permissions

Manifest V3 permissions remain `storage`, `tabs`, `notifications`,
`scripting`, `activeTab`, and `sidePanel`. API host permissions are limited to
the TrackMyOPT HTTPS domains; content-script match patterns cover job boards,
application systems, and career paths. The Store privacy form describes the
purpose of each permission. Remote code is **No**. All nine available user-data
categories are marked because optional profile/EEO data, credentials, salary
preferences, job-page content and URLs, interactions, IP/location, and feedback
can be processed. The three Limited Use declarations are checked. The website
policy explains the extension data flows in section 2.9.

Chrome warns that the host patterns may require in-depth review. This is a
review-time warning, not a validation failure.
