# Product

## Register

product

## Users

International students tracking OPT, STEM OPT, USCIS cases, and job applications.
They need clear information on a laptop or phone, including when a long wait or
an unfamiliar notice makes a case feel uncertain.

## Product Purpose

Help students understand their saved records, monitor changes, and find the next
useful action. Case status should be understood before historical comparisons
or detailed charts. Estimates must never look like official decisions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, professional, trustworthy. Use plain language and the existing TrackMyOPT
identity. Confidence comes from accurate information, not promises of outcomes.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded metric dashboards, oversized marketing panels inside task flows,
decorative motion, and competing warnings. Do not conceal uncertainty behind
polished charts or invent data to make a panel look complete.

## Design Principles

1. Show current status first and comparisons second. Keep the case-status
   "Explore detailed comparisons" section always open, with tabs for its charts.
2. Keep important warnings visible and actions easy to find.
3. Preserve the existing color palette and light/dark theme support.
4. Distinguish official status, user-entered dates, and historical estimates.
5. Make the default view compact without removing access to useful detail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable text, narrow screens, and
reduced motion. Use words alongside state colors so color is never the only cue.
