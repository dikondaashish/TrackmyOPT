# OPT planning resource pack

Reviewed October 10, 2026. Prepared for international student offices and student organizations. Distribution draft; no messages have been sent.

## Student-facing copy

Waiting for your OPT or STEM OPT application? Start with a clear explanation of the process, then use a free planning tool if it helps.

- [OPT processing guide](https://www.trackmyopt.com/blog/opt-processing-time-2026?utm_source=campus_resource_pack&utm_medium=partner&utm_campaign=opt_planning_2026&utm_content=opt_guide): where to check official USCIS estimates and what premium processing covers.
- [STEM OPT processing guide](https://www.trackmyopt.com/blog/stem-opt-processing-time-2026?utm_source=campus_resource_pack&utm_medium=partner&utm_campaign=opt_planning_2026&utm_content=stem_guide): processing, premium requests, and the extension rule with linked sources.
- [Free decision-window tool](https://www.trackmyopt.com/tools/opt-decision-window?utm_source=campus_resource_pack&utm_medium=partner&utm_campaign=opt_planning_2026&utm_content=decision_tool): compare your wait with completed community reports where enough comparable cases exist. The first lookup requires no account. Enter only the received date and optional three-letter receipt prefix, never a full receipt number.

Community reports are not a representative sample of all USCIS applications. Estimates are for planning, not promised decision dates. TrackMyOPT is an independent product, not USCIS or a university adviser. Use official USCIS information and your DSO for decisions about your individual situation. Account-based tracking is separate from the guest tool; paid features are optional.

## Small pilot and measurement

Start with five existing, appropriate campus relationships after the owner identifies or approves recipients. Personalize the introduction; do not imply an existing relationship or endorsement. Do not scrape student addresses, enroll recipients into recurring mail, or send applicant information.

Keep `utm_campaign=opt_planning_2026` and `utm_medium=partner`. For an approved institution, replace `utm_source=campus_resource_pack` with a public institution slug; never put a student's name, email, receipt number, or other private identifier in a URL.

Measure tagged landing visitors, tool results, signup, and seven-day activation through the same consent-observed funnel. Report raw counts alongside rates and wait for complete conversion windows. After four weeks, expand only if contacts find the resource useful and visitors activate. The generic links above identify the resource pack, not a particular university.
