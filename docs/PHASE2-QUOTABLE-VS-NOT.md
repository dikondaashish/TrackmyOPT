# Phase 2: Quotable vs Not — Question Priority Matrix

**Goal:** Identify which 50 questions will most likely drive AI citations.

**Key Rule:** Not all questions are created equal. AI models prefer:
1. **Definitive answers** (clear, concise, measurable)
2. **Data-backed answers** (statistics, rankings, comparisons)
3. **Procedural answers** (step-by-step, "how-to")
4. **Definitions** (complex terms explained simply)
5. **Comparisons** (A vs B tables, trade-offs)

Avoid (low AI citation probability):
- Opinion-based questions
- Vague questions without clear answers
- Questions where government/official sources dominate (hard to outrank)
- Questions about things that change monthly (unless you update daily)
