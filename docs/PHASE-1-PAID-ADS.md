## Campaign Optimization Rules

**Pause Rules:**
- Pause any ad with CPC > $5 for 7 days
- Pause any campaign with 0 conversions after $200 spend
- Pause any ad with CTR < 0.5% after 1,000 impressions

**Scaling Rules:**
- Scale winning campaigns by 20-30% per week (if ROAS > 3:1)
- Scale across similar audiences (test new audience segments)
- Move budget from underperformers to winners

**Daily Monitoring:**
- Check spend vs. budget (ensure not overspending)
- Monitor conversion costs (should be $25-50)
- Review new leads (quality check)
- Respond to leads within 1 hour

## Creative Testing Framework

**A/B Test Variables:**

For each campaign, test:
- **Headline:** A (Benefit-focused) vs. B (Curiosity-focused)
- **Ad copy:** A (Long-form) vs. B (Short-form)
- **CTA:** A (Free trial) vs. B (Download guide)
- **Image:** A (People-focused) vs. B (Data/metrics-focused)
- **Audience:** A (Broad interest) vs. B (Narrow interest)
- **Placement:** A (Desktop) vs. B (Mobile)

Test 1 variable at a time. Run for 2-3 weeks before conclusion.
