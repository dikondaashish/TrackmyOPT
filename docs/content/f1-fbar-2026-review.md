### Statements requiring individual professional review

All personal filing conclusions depend on immigration compliance, prior exempt history, actual presence, citizenship/green cards, exceptions, residency dates and elections. Arjun and Michael assume no separate residency basis. Priya and Sara assume qualifying exclusions have ended, countable presence meets the test, and no continued student exception applies. Daniel's 2026 conclusion excludes an eligible first-year choice; his 2027 conclusion assumes continued presence and account values. Treaties, spousal elections, dual-status years, missing Forms 8843, account authority, pensions, non-simultaneous maxima, delinquent reports and any special deadline relief require review of actual records. The article makes no claim that an attorney or CPA has reviewed these fictional students.
