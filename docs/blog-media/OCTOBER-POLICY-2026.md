## Evidence and limitations

- Grace period: Federal Register proposed rule 2026-18631 and current
  8 CFR 214.1(l)(2). A comment deadline is not an effective date.
- Fees: official August 25 PDF confirms September 24 comments. The September
  10 correction changes Table 13, not the legal status. September 18
  proclamation and plaintiffs' current Global Nurse Force litigation page
  are labeled separately. University of Michigan corroborates the court
  status. Recheck operative orders and agency guidance before a live filing.
- Employer scrutiny: September 18 executive order, especially sections 2–3.
  Agency directions do not establish that every employer with layoffs is barred.
- TN/TD: State Department September 18 announcement effective October 1.
  Preserve the distinction between consular visa applications and Canadian
  visa-exempt admission requests; TD applicants are expressly included.
- PERM: the announcement is attributed to Reuters and ABC reporting; Microsoft's
  response is labeled a company statement. Program mechanics use 20 CFR
  656.1 and 656.30. Individual company notices and later-stage implementation
  were not reviewed. POLITICO's user-supplied URL remains additional reading;
  it was inaccessible to the research tool, so no unsupported claim is derived
  from it. No company or applicant is described as guilty based on allegations.
