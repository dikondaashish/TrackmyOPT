Something I kept hearing from students on OPT: the big job sites are full of reposted, expired and staffing-agency listings, and it's easy to lose a week applying to roles that were never really open.

So we built Verified Jobs. It pulls openings directly from the career pages of 1,200+ US employers and keeps only US-based roles. You can filter by the experience a job asks for, show only employers with a past H-1B filing history (that's history, not a promise they'll sponsor you), and hide jobs that mention words like "citizenship" or "clearance".

Here's how applying works:

  1. Find a job in Verified Jobs. {{linkIntro}} Open Verified Jobs
  2. Click "Apply on ATS" to open the employer's own application page.
  3. Our Chrome extension fills in the form from your profile and can draft answers to the screening questions. You check everything and click submit yourself.
