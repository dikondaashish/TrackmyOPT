Something I kept hearing from students on OPT: the big job sites are full of reposted, expired and staffing-agency listings, and it's easy to lose a week applying to roles that were never really open.

So we built Verified Jobs. It pulls openings directly from the career pages of 1,200+ US employers and keeps only US-based roles. A few things that help on OPT:

  • Filter by how many years of experience a job asks for
  • Show only employers with a past H-1B filing history (that's history, not a promise they'll sponsor you)
  • Hide any job that mentions words like "citizenship" or "clearance"
