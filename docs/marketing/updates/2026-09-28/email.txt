Hi there,

Just kidding—this is a TrackMyOPT product update, not a USCIS notice.

Over September 15–24, we improved the tools that support your OPT journey and job search. Here’s what’s new on the website and what we’ve built for the next Chrome extension release.

01. A bigger Chrome extension update.

We built a guided first-run tour, a steadier side panel with stoppable resume progress, saved job-specific resumes you can restore later, and Undo last Prefill. On an explicit Prefill click, supported forms can use your saved profile and private answers; supported login pages can fill saved credentials. The extension can also prepare screening-answer drafts for you to review. We improved job-tracker saving and prefill across several application portals.

You stay in control of your answers and final submission. Some features depend on your plan. Rollout note: Chrome Web Store availability is being confirmed; your installed version may not include these improvements yet.

Explore Chrome Job Prefill:
https://www.trackmyopt.com/dashboard/extension?utm_source=product_update&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026_09_28

02. OPT dates with more clarity.

We refreshed the OPT Dates dashboard and the four OPT/STEM tools. Saved versus unsaved dates are clearer, unemployment-day calculations were tightened, and the STEM DSO recommendation date now has its own field. Employer suggestions and employment history gained company logos.

Review your OPT dates:
https://www.trackmyopt.com/dashboard/opt-dates?utm_source=product_update&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026_09_28

03. See your case and next actions together.

Case milestones, monitoring status, and four comparison views are now easier to scan together. You can record biometrics you attended, organize notices and confirmed deadlines, export dates to your calendar, and mark tasks complete. Official processing information is shown separately from historical community reports.

For paid members: deadline emails and the weekly case digest are opt-in.

Explore your case view:
https://www.trackmyopt.com/dashboard/case-status?utm_source=product_update&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026_09_28

04. Find people. Write a thoughtful note.

The new Networking workspace helps you check for an available work email from a LinkedIn profile and draft a personal email or LinkedIn note. Outreach bundles combine contact discovery and drafts in an optional pilot. Results depend on available data; you review and send any message yourself.

Try Networking:
https://www.trackmyopt.com/dashboard/career/networking?utm_source=product_update&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026_09_28

05. A smoother Document Vault.

A more compact layout puts your files first, especially on mobile. Uploads, downloads, preview retries, expiry-date edits, and reminder settings now give clearer feedback.

Document Vault is a paid-plan feature.
