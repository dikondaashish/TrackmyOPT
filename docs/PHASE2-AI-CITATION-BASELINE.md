## How to Record Results

**For each query:**

1. Search in **ChatGPT** (no plugins, just regular chat with Browse)
2. Screenshot or copy the cited sources
3. Record in the table:
   - Source domain (e.g., "USCIS.gov", "VisaVerge.com", "TrackMyOPT.com")
   - **If TrackMyOPT:** ✅ Mark as cited
   - **If competitor:** Note which page (blog post, tool, feature)
   - **If government/law:** Note why they're preferred (domain authority, official status)

4. Repeat for **Perplexity AI** and **Google Gemini**

**Example:**
| 1 | "What is OPT?" | USCIS.gov, Wikipedia | VisaVerge blog | USCIS.gov | Baseline: USCIS preferred for definitions. TrackMyOPT not cited. |
